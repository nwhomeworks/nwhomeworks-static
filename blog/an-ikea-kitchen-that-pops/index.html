An IKEA Kitchen That Pops!

September 9, 2013 • By Tim Hutchinson • IKEA Hacks

Here are some pictures from our latest kitchen remodel for some clients in Kirkland. Check out the bathroom remodel we did for the same clients here: Master Bath Remodel – Give Me Space!

IKEA doesn’t make a base corner cabinet with an angled front like this, but we were able to do a simple modification to their lazy susan cabinet which allows us to use this corner sink. That kind of flexibility is one reason we specialize in IKEA kitchen installation. The 17″ door from IKEA’s wall corner cabinet fits perfectly here. [Update 2026 – IKEA doesn’t make a 17″ corner door anymore with their new Sektion line, but this hack still works with an 18″ door]

This little wet bar area is oh-so-cool and a great addition to any kitchen remodeling project. The under cabinet LED lights really make things pop! The wine shelf is custom made using wall cover panels.
