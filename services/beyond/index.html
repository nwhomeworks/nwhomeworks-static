Beyond Kitchen & Bath

Basements, built-ins, laundry rooms and storage — the same crew, the same standards

Kitchens and bathrooms are our specialty, but great craftsmanship doesn’t stop at the kitchen door. Over the years our clients have asked us to bring the same standards to the rest of the house — and these are the rooms that come up most.

The same carpenters, tile setters, electricians, and plumbers who build our kitchens and our award-winning bathrooms do this work too. That matters most below grade, where a basement conversion means running plumbing and dedicated circuits into a floor that never had them — and it shows up in the small rooms too, like a laundry room and mudroom where a couple of inches decide whether the space works. The same carpenters build our custom built-ins and closets, where the finish work is the whole job. Whatever the room, it’s one contractor, one crew, and one point of contact.
