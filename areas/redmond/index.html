Kitchen Remodeling in Redmond

This kitchen was designed around how the family actually cooks. The Blue Star range is the centerpiece, but the details do the daily work: a wall-mounted double oven at the right height, deep drawers where pots actually live, under-cabinet lighting over every prep surface, and open shelves that keep the everyday dishes one reach away.

That’s our approach everywhere — start with how you want the space to work, then build to it. If you’re weighing options or just want a realistic number, our kitchen remodel cost estimator is a good place to start. See our full kitchen remodeling services →

Custom Storage, Built In

Look at the appliance garage in this photo: the toaster, the coffee maker, and the blender all live behind a lifting door on the counter, plugged in and ready, invisible when you’re done. It’s a small piece of cabinetry that changes how the whole counter feels every single day.

Because we build cabinetry and do the electrical ourselves, details like this don’t require three subcontractors to coordinate — they just get built, correctly, the first time. That’s what keeps a remodel feeling custom instead of catalog.

Bathroom Remodeling in Redmond

Bathrooms are where in-house trades matter most. Plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tile, cabinetry, and glass all have to come together in a few dozen square feet, and nothing gets lost between subcontractors when one crew does all of it. Our bathroom work earned a 2026 Chrysalis Award — a national remodeling honor — and the same crew that built that bathroom builds yours.

From a full primary-suite overhaul to a hall bath where the budget has to work harder, we bring the same standards to every project. See our full bathroom remodeling services →
