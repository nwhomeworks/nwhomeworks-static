Your West Seattle Remodeling Partner

That view up top isn’t a stock photo — it’s the view from the street outside one of our recent bathroom remodels, looking out over Puget Sound to the Olympics. West Seattle is full of homes like that: Craftsman bungalows near the Junction, mid-century view homes up in Admiral and Gatewood, solid brick houses in Genesee. Great bones, great locations — and kitchens and bathrooms that haven’t been touched in decades.

We’ve been over here a lot lately — both projects on this page are recent West Seattle jobs. We handle every trade in-house — framing, electrical, plumbing, tile, cabinetry, and finish carpentry — which means one contractor, one point of contact, and no gaps between subs.

Kitchen Remodeling in West Seattle

This green AXSTAD kitchen tells you a lot about how we like to work. The homeowner started his own IKEA install and stalled out partway through — so we got him unstuck. We rebuilt the peninsula corner so every drawer opens freely, ran the cabinets to the ceiling with trimmed panels for a built-in look, finished the electrical and lighting, vented the range hood through the roof, and set the built-in oven. He got his second wind and tiled the herringbone backsplash himself. Read the full story of this kitchen here.

Whether you want us start to finish, or just need a pro to carry an IKEA kitchen installation over the finish line, the same crew and the same standards show up. See our full kitchen remodeling services →

Bathroom Remodeling in West Seattle

This is the bathroom with that view outside the front door. The owners wanted something warm and modern, so we ran vertically stacked terracotta tile floor to ceiling, set a trio of tiled niches into the shower wall, and paired it with matte black fixtures, a fluted white-oak vanity, and large-format stone-look floor tile. Small room, big presence.

Bathrooms are where in-house trades matter most — plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tile, and glass all have to come together in a few dozen square feet, and nothing gets lost between subcontractors when it’s one crew doing all of it. See our full bathroom remodeling services →

Built Properly, Down to the Details

Look closer at that shower wall: three recessed niches, each one framed in black edge trim, each course of tile lining up through the openings. That kind of detail doesn’t happen by accident — it’s planned before the first tile goes on, and it’s backed by proper waterproofing underneath.

It’s the same care we bring to basements, built-ins, laundry rooms, and whole-home projects across West Seattle. If your home has a space that isn’t working, it’s worth a conversation. See our full range of services →
